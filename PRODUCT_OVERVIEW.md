# MacFiesta Pro — Product Overview

**MacFiesta Pro** is the production platform replacing the legacy MacFiesta website and spreadsheet-based fest operations for MACFAST.

It provides a public fest site, participant accounts and registration, desk payment / verification workflows, and staff tools for events, results, gallery, announcements, CMS, and operational reports.

> Historical MCA / viva materials live under `docs/archive/`. **This product is for live festival use.**

**Current feature inventory and official launch checklist:** [PRODUCTION_STATUS.md](./PRODUCTION_STATUS.md)

---

## Objectives (production)

1. Replace the old public MacFiesta site with a maintainable React + Django stack.
2. Let participants create accounts, register for events, and receive registration numbers / QR passes.
3. Let finance and verification desks update payment and attendance without spreadsheets as the source of truth.
4. Let organizers manage events, schedule, results, gallery, announcements, and homepage CMS content.
5. Export CSV reports for Food, Hospitality, Transport, and Core Team planning.
6. Keep heavy ERP modules deferred until operations need them — see [FUTURE_ENHANCEMENTS_STATUS.md](./FUTURE_ENHANCEMENTS_STATUS.md).
